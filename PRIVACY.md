# Privacy policy: Keep Tab Active

Last updated: 2026-10-04

Keep Tab Active collects no data. It does not store, transmit, sell, or share any information about you or your browsing.

What it does touch, and only inside your browser:

- **The tab you protect.** It sets that tab's "auto discardable" flag so the browser's Memory Saver skips it. The flag lives on the tab and disappears when the tab closes.
- **That tab's favicon.** To show a pin badge, it reads the site's icon from the browser's own favicon cache, draws a badge on it, and swaps it into the page. This happens on your device; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Page access.** By default it can only reach the tab you are viewing, at the moment you protect it. If you turn on "Allow access to all sites" in its options, it uses that access for the same favicon swap only, on protected tabs.

The extension makes no network requests, uses no analytics, and has no server.
